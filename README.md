## About Me

I'm a **Senior AI Security & Compliance Engineer** and the **Founder of [Sether](https://setherai.vercel.app/#sandbox) (Raeven, Inc.)** — the streaming PII redaction layer that sits between AI applications and LLM providers. I'm an **agile software engineer with 7+ years of experience** building innovative, scalable solutions across AI, fintech, mobile, and security. ## What I do, in plain language

- **Mobile:** Production **Flutter / Dart** — offline-first architectures, biometric encryption (Android Keystore / Secure Enclave), Shorebird OTA for zero-downtime hotfixes.
- **Web & Backend:** **TypeScript** across the stack — **NestJS** event-driven microservices, **Next.js 15** App Router, **Node.js**, **GraphQL**.
- **AI Engineering:** Production **RAG** pipelines, **LangChain / LangGraph**, **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**, local **ONNX** rerankers for sub-second latency, vector search with **Weaviate** and **pgvector**.
- **Security & Compliance:** **PII redaction** at the LLM sub-processor boundary, **supply-chain security** in **Rust** (`npm_sentinel`, `rust_secure_dependency_audit`), real **DPRK incident response** experience (PolinRider — forensics, cleaner, CI/CD hardening).
- **Cross-border regulatory surface:** I cover **GDPR Art. 28**, **SOC2 CC6.7**, **HIPAA §164.312**, **EU AI Act §10**, and **Nigeria's NDPA 2023** on the same products — few engineers sit in that exact chair.
